❌ BAD (Needs Improvement)
High Priority — Action Immediately
[GM-001] Technical errors shown as Game Master dialogue
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Exposes raw exception text and system terminology (legal strategy decision) directly to the player, which disrupts immersion and risks exposing internal architecture.

Fix: Replace raw tracebacks with a generic, polite failure message and optional support reference code:

"The shot could not be completed. Your ball and score have not changed. Please try again. If this continues, return to the hole and resume the round."

[GM-002] Unexplained 0–100 decision score comparison
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Presenting YOU 11/100 · GM 70/100 first tells players their choice was terrible without providing context, leading to frustration even when accompanied by a disclaimer.

Fix: Lead with evidence using a structured Result / Decision / Outcome vs Target breakdown. Hide or demote the raw 0–100 numerical grade behind a secondary "Why this grade?" toggle.

[GM-003] Declaring a 0% make-chance putt as a "sound make attempt"
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Displaying 0% make chance produced a miss alongside praise for a sound read/pace sounds contradictory, obvious, and demotivating.

Fix:

Change probability displays near zero to state "under 1%" rather than truncating/rounding to 0%.

Frame long putts around lag-putt success criteria (leave distance) rather than make rates:

"The read and pace were sound for a lag putt. From this distance, the modeled make chance was under 1%; the goal was to leave a manageable next putt."

[GM-004] Blending physical result with value judgment
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Prefaces like "Good shot" or "Costly miss" prematurely judge execution before separating intent, strategic risk, and physical outcome. Terms like "dispersed" reflect game engine mechanics rather than natural play.

Fix: Decouple observations into four clear, neutral categories: Result, Decision, Outcome vs Target, and Caddie Review. Never diagnose physical swing errors (e.g., open face) without hard sensor/input data.

[GM-005] Full-shot stance briefing grammar
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Redundant wording ("The stance has a fairly level stance...").

Fix: Clean up syntax to natural English:

"Your stance is fairly level, and the shot plays nearly level."

[GM-006] Technical jargon in fairness disclaimer
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Terms like "paired execution luck" sound like internal software parameters rather than natural caddie dialogue.

Fix: Rephrase to plain language:

"You and the Game Master use the same golfer profile and the same shot conditions. The comparison is designed to test the plans, not who receives the luckier result."

[GM-007] Excessive message stacking post-shot
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Sending 3 to 5 separate notification bubbles after a single shot creates UI clutter, especially on mobile screens.

Fix: Consolidate all post-shot feedback into a single primary summary card containing four distinct subsections. Place extended commentary behind a "More caddie detail" disclosure.

[GM-008] Unconfirmed automatic target selection
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Silently selecting a target when none was specified removes player agency and confuses post-shot reviews.

Fix: Require explicit player confirmation before executing the shot when a default target is populated.

[GM-009] Hyper-precise distance measurements
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Expressing adjustments in hundredths of a yard (1.99 yards left, 3.95 yards right) adds irrelevant precision that doesn't mirror real golf decision-making.

Fix: Round distance outputs to whole yards (or at most one decimal place when close to the target).

[GM-010] Misleading accuracy reward messaging
Verdict: BAD

Issue: Stating "7 Iron accuracy improves..." implies the player's core skill profile has permanently upgraded, rather than reflecting a one-time situational modifier.

Fix: Explicitly state that the adjustment applies only to the current modeled shot:

"Good adjustment. For this simulated shot, the model increases 7 Iron accuracy from 72% to 78%. Your saved golfer profile is unchanged."
